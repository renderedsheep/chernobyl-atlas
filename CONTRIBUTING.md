# Suggesting changes

You don't need to know Git to help. Open the atlas, find the object, and use the links at the bottom of its card, or click anywhere on the map for "suggest a site here". The Справка (about) tab has the same options. Each link opens a short form on GitHub, partly filled in for you. You need a free GitHub account.

The forms:
- **Correct a location**: something is in the wrong place.
- **Add a site**: a place that is missing.
- **Add a photo**: your own photo, or one under a free licence.
- **Other correction or idea**: wrong facts, broken links, anything else.

Every suggestion is public and is checked before the map changes.

## Ground rules
- Text and data you contribute are published under CC BY-SA 4.0, like the rest of the atlas content.
- Photos must be your own (shared under CC BY-SA 4.0) or already under a free licence, with a named photographer.
- Please give a source where you can: a link, a book, a map, or your own visit.
- No military positions, defences, minefields or security arrangements from 2022 onward, even if they have been reported elsewhere.
